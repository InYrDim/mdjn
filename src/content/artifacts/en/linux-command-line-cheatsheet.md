Print it, fold it, keep it next to the keyboard. This is the condensed version of
the commands that survive on my cheat wall:

- **Navigation** — `pwd`, `ls -la`, `cd -`, pushd/popd
- **Permissions** — `chmod`, `chown`, and the `ugoa` shorthand
- **Processes** — `ps aux`, `kill`, `htop`, job control with `Ctrl+Z`/`fg`/`bg`
- **Packages** — `apt search/install/purge` and `dpkg -l | grep`

The PDF is formatted for A4 so it also works as a wallpaper cheat sheet on a
second monitor. If you spot a command that deserves a spot, email me — the sheet
gets a revision every few months.
